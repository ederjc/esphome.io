The `bmm350` sensor platform measures magnetic field strength along three axes
and reports the sensor die temperature. An [I²C bus](/components/i2c) is required.

```yaml
sensor:
  - platform: bmm350
    field_strength_x:
      name: "Magnetic Field X"
    field_strength_y:
      name: "Magnetic Field Y"
    field_strength_z:
      name: "Magnetic Field Z"
    temperature:
      name: "Magnetometer Temperature"
```

## Configuration variables

- **address** (*Optional*, int): The I²C address. Either `0x14` (default) or `0x15`,
  depending on the state of the sensor's address-select pin.
- **field_strength_x** (*Optional*): Magnetic field along the sensor's X axis, in
  µT. Accepts all [sensor options](/components/sensor).
- **field_strength_y** (*Optional*): Magnetic field along the sensor's Y axis, in
  µT. Accepts all [sensor options](/components/sensor).
- **field_strength_z** (*Optional*): Magnetic field along the sensor's Z axis, in
  µT. Accepts all [sensor options](/components/sensor).
- **temperature** (*Optional*): Sensor die temperature, in °C. This is not ambient
  temperature. Accepts all [sensor options](/components/sensor).
- **update_interval** (*Optional*, [Time](/guides/configuration-types/#time)):
  How often to read and publish the measurements. Defaults to `60s` and must be
  at least `40ms`. This does not change the sensor's 25 Hz sampling rate or
  two-sample averaging.

Field measurements use the sensor's native axes and factory compensation.
The component does not compute heading or calibrate for nearby magnetic
materials.

## Infineon PSoC 6 AI Evaluation Kit

The onboard BMM350 on the CY8CKIT-062S2-AI connects directly to the board's
SCB0 I²C bus at `0x15`. Set `address: 0x15` and disable scanning on the I²C
bus with `scan: false`. Do not configure this sensor through the BMI270's
auxiliary bus or as a BMM150.
